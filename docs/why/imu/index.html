Why calibrate the IMU

This page shows what an IMU calibration gains a tracker downstream. Data: 24 headset recordings from the Monado SLAM Datasets, plus VI-Rig in the last section.

The answer first:

  • Mono gains most: correcting the IMU and timing with MVIS cuts the median OpenVINS mono SE3 ATE from 28.80 to 9.56 mm, by two thirds.
  • Stereo gains less: the median stereo SE3 ATE drops from 8.94 to 7.04 mm, and the 1 s drift improves on 24/24 recordings.
  • Online calibration does not replace it: on the as-published recordings, only stereo with online tdt_dtd​ ties the corrected recording.
  • Online calibration on top adds nothing: on the corrected recordings, leave it off.
  • VI-Rig shows the same kind of error: the T265 accel scale is off by 1.7–2.0 % on every axis.

How to turn it on: estimate the IMU intrinsics in Camera–IMU calibration, then write a corrected recording with mvis apply-calib.

The setup

Section titled “The setup”

We ran the OpenVINS visual-inertial tracker twice on each recording: once as published, once corrected by MVIS.

Recordings: 24 imucalib recordings from the Monado SLAM Datasets:

HeadsetRecordings
Valve Index8
HP Reverb G212
Samsung Odyssey+4

Two arms: the two runs of a recording differ only in the input.

  • As published: the recording as the dataset ships it.
  • Corrected: the same recording after mvis apply-calib with the MVIS calibration. It applies:
    • the IMU intrinsics Dw\mathbf{D}_wDw​ and Da\mathbf{D}_aDa​, the gyro and accel scale and misalignment;
    • removal of the static bias;
    • each camera’s camera–IMU time offset tdt_dtd​, by shifting the image stamps;
    • the gyro–accel time offset tgat_{ga}tga​, by re-interpolating the accelerometer.
  • Images: unchanged.

Shared by both arms:

  • the same MVIS camera intrinsics (kb4) and camera–IMU extrinsics;
  • identical OpenVINS configs, byte for byte.

So the test measures one thing: the MVIS IMU and time correction, given the MVIS geometry.

Ground truth: the lighthouse poses that come with the dataset.

Metrics:

  • SE3 ATE (mm): position error after a rigid alignment to the ground truth. This is the primary metric.
  • RPE 1 s (mm): position drift over 1 s windows.
  • Scale error (%): ∣s−1∣\lvert s - 1 \rvert∣s−1∣, where sss is the scale of a similarity (Sim3) alignment. It shows how far the trajectory size is off.
  • Medians and wins: each number is a median over recordings. “Better” counts the recordings where the corrected recording wins.
  • p: Wilcoxon signed-rank p, paired by recording, not corrected for multiple tests.

2. Online calibration on the published recordings does not catch up

Section titled “2. Online calibration on the published recordings does not catch up”

OpenVINS can estimate some calibration online: one tdt_dtd​ for all cameras, the IMU intrinsics and the camera–IMU extrinsics. Here the corrected recording with nothing online meets the as-published recording with online estimation on. SE3 ATE in mm, medians:

4. On VI-Rig, the T265 accel scale is off on every axis

Section titled “4. On VI-Rig, the T265 accel scale is off on every axis”

The same kind of error shows on our own rig, virig-4imus-4cams. We take the ELP stereo + two IMUs run (T8 in the results), 15 recordings:

  • Rig: ELP stereo, the T265 IMU as base IMU, the Xsens as aux IMU.
  • IMU model: MVIS with Kalibr’s IMU convention (kalibr), the “MVIS (K-IMU)” column.

Accel scales, the diagonal of Da\mathbf{D}_aDa​, medians:

IMUDa\mathbf{D}_aDa​ xDa\mathbf{D}_aDa​ yDa\mathbf{D}_aDa​ z
T265 IMU1.019891.019051.01668

  • T265 IMU: accel scale off by 1.7–2.0 % on every axis.
  • Xsens: within 0.1 % of 1, a check on the T265 result.
  • Same fix: the corrected Monado recordings apply Da\mathbf{D}_aDa​ and Dw\mathbf{D}_wDw​ the same way.

Conclusion:

  • Correct the IMU before the tracker: it cuts mono SE3 ATE by two thirds and helps stereo too.
  • Online calibration is no substitute: it does not catch up on the as-published recordings. On the corrected ones it adds nothing, so leave it off.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • In-sample: MVIS was solved on the same recordings. No held-out check yet.
  • Shared geometry: both arms use the MVIS camera intrinsics and extrinsics. This is not MVIS against the best calibration for the as-published data.
  • ”As published” is not raw: the dataset already carries its own Basalt IMU correction.
  • Valve corrected twice: on Valve that correction was applied twice, which leaves an accel scale error of about 6 %. Part of the Valve mono gain likely undoes that.
  • One combined gain: the correction fixes IMU intrinsics, bias, td and t_ga together. Their effects were not separated.
  • Too-large trajectories: Reverb G2 and Odyssey+ trajectories come out about 1–2 % too large in both arms. The cause is not known.
  • One recording gets worse: Odyssey+ MOC11 stereo goes from 11.4 to 16.6 mm with the correction, though its RPE and mono improve.
  • Small group: Odyssey+ has only 4 pairs.
  • Priors not tuned: the OpenVINS online priors were left at fixed values.
