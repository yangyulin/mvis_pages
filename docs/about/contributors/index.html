Citing the leaderboard

Section titled “Citing the leaderboard”

Leaderboard snapshots are versioned so a comparison stays citable after later submissions land. Cite the snapshot, not the live page:

Open MVIS leaderboard, batch 2026-11-04a, accessed <date>. https://openmvis.com/benchmark/leaderboard/

See Methodology for what the numbers mean.

Citing the datasets

Section titled “Citing the datasets”

Each released dataset has its own citation and DOI, given on its page — see Datasets. Cite the dataset in addition to the toolbox when your results depend on it.

Contributing

Section titled “Contributing”
