Section titled “Demo and values”

Head-worn stereo fisheye camera with a 6-axis IMU, recording to a memory card. Borme, a brand of Shenzhen Feikasi Industrial Co., Ltd., sells it as the XQ20. Two units were calibrated.

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

No independent reference. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

The path of the rig around the board, as MVIS solved it. To explore it:

  • Press Play or drag the bar to follow the path.
  • Use the view buttons to change the view.
  • Pick a camera card to put that camera on the frustum, the pyramid that shows its view.

Each card draws the board from the solved pose, with the corners MVIS detected. We publish no camera images.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • No independent reference. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.
  • The supplier's IMU noise file (gyro 0.002, accel 0.02) is tuned for Kalibr, about 10× and 3× the noise measured at rest. Used as is in MVIS, it put unit A's lever arm 20 to 45 mm too far forward. These results use the measured noise with a 2 to 3× margin.
  • With IMU intrinsics free, the lever arm's y shifts about 9 mm on both units, more than its 3.5 mm uncertainty. Pinned, it agrees with the supplier's Kalibr, so these results pin the IMU intrinsics.
  • The listing says 1080P and 160°, and the supplier's sheet says 1920×1200 and 123.6°. These recordings are 1920×1200. The listing's shutter value wasn't tested here.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • cam0 at 30 Hz, cam1 at 10 Hz, t_ga estimated, camera intrinsics free, IMU intrinsics pinned. IMU noise padded from the measured noise: gyro 6e-4 rad/s/√Hz, accel 0.015 m/s²/√Hz. Batch of 2026-11-07.
