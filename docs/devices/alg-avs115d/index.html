Section titled “Demo and values”

GMSL2 stereo camera module from Shenzhen Aili-Light Technology (ALG Tech, 艾利光), with two 1920×1920 rolling-shutter cameras 65 mm apart and a 6-axis IMU. One hand-held recording in front of a ChArUco board, solved as stereo + IMU with each camera's readout time estimated.

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

One recording, with no independent reference on this page. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

The path of the rig around the board, as MVIS solved it. To explore it:

  • Press Play or drag the bar to follow the path.
  • Use the view buttons to change the view.
  • Pick a camera card to put that camera on the frustum, the pyramid that shows its view.

Each card draws the board from the solved pose, with the corners MVIS detected. We publish no camera images.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • One recording, with no independent reference on this page. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.
  • The camera-only solve, without the IMU, puts the readout at 25 ms against 31 ms here; the board's motion alone pins it less well, so the joint value is the one shown.
  • In a simulated copy of this recording, with noise matched to this unit, MVIS recovered the lever arm within 1.2 mm, the rotation within 0.3 mrad, the time offset within 0.5 ms, the readout within 0.6 ms and the gyro scales within 0.7 %. With MVIS's default IMU noise the same test drifts (lever arm 6 mm along the optical axis, gyro scales 1 to 1.6 %), which is why this solve uses the tighter noise.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • Both cameras and the IMU, rolling shutter with each camera's readout time estimated, Kannala–Brandt (kb4) lenses with intrinsics free, IMU noise set about 5× tighter than MVIS's default to match this unit, t_ga estimated. Some frames were blurred by fast motion; stereo pairs whose board edges were more than 1.5× wider than the median were left out (17 % of the pairs). The IMU stored accel 4× and gyro 2× too small; both were scaled before solving. Solved 2026-11-14.
