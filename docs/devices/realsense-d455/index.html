Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

Depth camera from RealSense with a 95 mm global-shutter infrared stereo pair, a global-shutter RGB camera and a 6-axis IMU. One hand-held recording in front of a chessboard on a screen, solved as left infrared + right infrared + RGB + IMU.

Reprojection median0.07 px
cam0–cam1 baseline94.4 mm
cam0 td+7.16 ms
cam0 lever arm33.1 mm

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

One recording, with no independent reference on this page. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

The path of the rig around the board, as MVIS solved it. To explore it:

  • Press Play or drag the bar to follow the path.
  • Use the view buttons to change the view.
  • Pick a camera card to put that camera on the frustum, the pyramid that shows its view.

Each card draws the board from the solved pose, with the corners MVIS detected. We publish no camera images.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • One recording, with no independent reference on this page. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.
  • The square size is the recorder's own measurement of a board shown on a screen. It scales every baseline and lever arm; the solved stereo baseline comes out about 0.7 % under the 95 mm the maker lists, which a square of about 75.5 mm would explain. Not confirmed.
  • The infrared images are the camera's own rectified output, so their distortion is close to zero by construction and the values describe that output, not the bare lens.
  • The recorder turned the camera's IMU correction off, so the IMU scale and bias values are those of the raw sensor. The accelerometer was resampled onto the gyro's time stamps before the solve.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • Left infrared, right infrared and RGB cameras with the IMU in one solve, all three treated as global shutter. Pinhole camera with radial-tangential distortion, seeded from three single-camera solves of the same recording and left free; camera time offsets, IMU scale and misalignment and the gyro-to-accel offset t_ga estimated. The board is kept rigid. IMU noise is the MVIS default, since no measured values exist for this unit. Solved 2026-11-13.
