Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

GMSL2 stereo camera module from Shenzhen Aili-Light Technology (ALG Tech, 艾利光), with two global-shutter cameras 50 mm apart and a 6-axis IMU. Three recordings, each solved as stereo + IMU; this page shows the longest. The recordings match ALG's AVS111C-Y; the exact model isn't confirmed by the maker.

Reprojection median0.11 px
cam0–cam1 baseline49.9 mm
cam0 td−16.32 ms
cam0 lever arm46.6 mm

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

No independent reference, so these values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

The path of the rig around the board, as MVIS solved it. To explore it:

  • Press Play or drag the bar to follow the path.
  • Use the view buttons to change the view.
  • Pick a camera card to put that camera on the frustum, the pyramid that shows its view.

Each card draws the board from the solved pose, with the corners MVIS detected. We publish no camera images.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • No independent reference, so these values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.
  • We solved three recordings of the same unit separately. The lever arm agrees within 3 mm, the camera-to-IMU time offset within 0.2 ms, the focal lengths within 1.3 px and the IMU scales within 0.4 %. The IMU position also agrees with the maker's drawing within 2 mm.
  • The board reaches about 54° off-axis. Past that, toward the image edges (61°) and corners (72°), the lens model is extrapolated: the three solves differ by up to 7 px at the edges and 35 px at the corners.
  • The motion is gentle (about 6 to 11°/s RMS). In a simulated copy of each recording, with noise matched to this unit, MVIS recovered the lever arm within 1.1 mm, the rotation within 0.6 mrad, the time offset within 0.1 ms and the IMU scales within 0.4 %. The solver's own σ for the lever arm (2 to 4 mm) is wider, because the solve used MVIS's default IMU noise, which is looser than this IMU needs.
  • The model is matched from resolution, baseline, field of view and shutter, not confirmed by the maker.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • Both cameras and the IMU, global shutter, default IMU noise, t_ga estimated, camera intrinsics free. The IMU stored accel 4× and gyro 2× too small; both were scaled before solving. Batch of 2026-11-11.
