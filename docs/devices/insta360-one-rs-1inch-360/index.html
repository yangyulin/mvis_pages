Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

Consumer 360° camera from Insta360 with two 1-inch sensors behind back-to-back fisheye lenses of about 200° each and a built-in IMU at 1 kHz. It is the camera of the Hilti-Trimble SLAM Challenge 2026 dataset. Each lens is solved with the IMU from the organisers' own calibration recording, with the rolling-shutter readout estimated.

No video on this page. The values below come from the solved run.

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

The two lenses are solved in separate recordings, so the distance between them comes from two camera-to-IMU results, not from a direct solve of the pair. The lenses face opposite ways and rarely see the board together.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

The path of the rig around the board, as MVIS solved it. To explore it:

  • Press Play or drag the bar to follow the path.
  • Use the view buttons to change the view.
  • Pick a camera card to put that camera on the frustum, the pyramid that shows its view.

Each card draws the board from the solved pose, with the corners MVIS detected. We publish no camera images.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • The two lenses are solved in separate recordings, so the distance between them comes from two camera-to-IMU results, not from a direct solve of the pair. The lenses face opposite ways and rarely see the board together.
  • The organisers publish a Kalibr calibration (EUCM model, IMU intrinsics fixed). It isn't an independent reference: it comes from the same recordings.
  • The board reaches about 95° from the lens axis, so the outermost part of each 200° image is extrapolated.
  • The images are JPEG-compressed, which can shift detected corners slightly.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • Each lens alone with the IMU. Camera intrinsics (Kannala–Brandt kb4) start from a single-camera solve of the camera-pair recording and stay free; rolling-shutter readout, camera time offset, IMU scale and misalignment and the gyro-to-accel offset t_ga estimated. IMU noise measured on the camera-pair recording, where the camera stands still, and widened about 2.8×. Solved 2026-11-12.
