Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

VR headset from Samsung with two 640×480 grayscale tracking cameras and an IMU at about 1 kHz, solved from the Monado SLAM calibration data.

Reprojection median0.12 px
cam0–cam1 baseline105.6 mm
cam0 td−0.65 ms
cam0 lever arm84.2 mm

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

One recording shown, with no independent reference on this page. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

The path of the rig around the board, as MVIS solved it. To explore it:

  • Press Play or drag the bar to follow the path.
  • Use the view buttons to change the view.
  • Pick a camera card to put that camera on the frustum, the pyramid that shows its view.

Each card draws the board from the solved pose, with the corners MVIS detected. We publish no camera images.

Detailed results

Section titled “Detailed results”
Lens Calibrated field of view

Calibrated field of view, horizontal × vertical. cam0: 119° × 95°, corners reach 73° from the axis · cam1: 119° × 94°, corners reach 69° from the axis

cam0 cam1

We read these from the calibrated lens fit at the image edges, through the principal point (where the optical axis meets the image). ~ marks an axis past where the corners reached, so the fit is extrapolated there. Past the fit means an edge lies beyond where the fit holds, so we give no value.

IMU Gyro–accel time offset t_ga +2.20 ms

Each bar shows a scale as its deviation from 1. IMU model: calyx. t_ga, the time offset between the gyroscope and accelerometer channels: +2.20 ± 0.13 ms.
