Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • No independent reference. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.
  • Recording 1 lost frames on both cameras at decode, so it has gaps.
  • Recording 2 is solved from the blurred video. Blurring moved its lever arm by 0.07 mm.
  • On cam0 the horizontal image edges lie past what the lens fit can describe, so that field of view has no solved figure.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • Recording converted to EuRoC layout, camera-only solve per camera, then camera + IMU with camera intrinsics free and t_ga on. Batch of 2026-11-03.
