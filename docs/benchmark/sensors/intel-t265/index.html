Tracking camera from Intel with two global-shutter fisheye cameras and a Bosch BMI055 IMU, solved here on its own IMU from one recording of our VI-Rig.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • One recording, with no independent reference on this page. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.
  • The T265 gyro-to-accel offset isn't a pure time shift. We measured about −6.8 ms at 2–4 Hz and −1.2 ms at 30–40 Hz, so a single t_ga is a compromise that depends on the motion.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • Fisheye stereo plus the T265's own IMU, camera intrinsics free, t_ga on. Batch of 2026-11-03.
