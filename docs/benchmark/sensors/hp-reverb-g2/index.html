Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

VR headset from HP with four 640×480 grayscale tracking cameras and an IMU at about 1 kHz. All four are solved from the Monado SLAM calibration data, in three pair recordings stitched together.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

The solved path of the rig around the board. Press Play or drag the bar to follow it, use the buttons to change the view, and pick a camera card to put that camera on the frustum. Each card draws the board from the solved pose with the corners MVIS detected; no camera image is published.

Limits

Section titled “Limits”
  • One recording shown, with no independent reference on this page. These values show what MVIS solved, not how close they are to the truth.
  • This rig is stitched from three solves, not one joint solve. Cameras seen in two pairs agree to 0.89 mm and 0.34° (cam0) and 0.54 mm and 0.15° (cam1). cam2 and cam3 move 0.79 and 0.52 mm depending on how they are attached.
  • The process figure and values for the IMU show the cam0 + cam1 recording only.

How it was run

Section titled “How it was run”
  • Each recording shows the board to one camera pair, so three pairs are solved: cam0 + cam1, cam0 + cam2, cam1 + cam3. Each is camera + IMU with camera intrinsics free and t_ga on, on current MVIS, 2026-11-03. cam0, cam1 and all IMU values come from the first pair. cam2 is attached through cam0 and cam3 through cam1.
