Section titled “Detailed results”
Lens Calibrated field of view

Calibrated field of view, horizontal × vertical. cam0: 120° × 120°, corners reach 62° from the axis · cam1: ~119° × 119°, corners reach 61° from the axis

cam0 cam1

Read from the calibrated lens fit at the image edges, through the principal point. ~ marks an axis past the range the corners reached, where the fit is extrapolated. Past the fit means an edge lies beyond where the fit holds, so no value is given.
