Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

One MVIS camera + IMU run on hand-held recording 1: the two mono cameras, the rolling-shutter colour camera and its readout time, solved together.

Reprojection median0.17 px
cam0–cam1 baseline99.2 mm
cam0 td+1.24 ms
cam0 lever arm43.9 mm

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

Section titled “Calibration process”

MVIS's own player, from this run: the detected board corners, then the rig's path as the solve refines it. Press Play or drag the bar; pick a camera card to look through that camera. We publish no camera images.

Lever arms and rotations are each sensor in the base IMU frame; rotation is log(R) in rad. td is the time offset to the base IMU, t_imu = t_sensor + td. Reprojection is the median per camera (all cameras: median 0.175 px): how well the model fits the corners, not how accurate it is.

Config and run

Section titled “Config and run”

The marked lines are the choices that matter for this rig:

  • camera_input.free_intrinsics: Camera intrinsics stay free in the camera + IMU solve.
  • camera_input.cameras: The colour camera (cam2) has a rolling shutter, so its readout time is estimated.
  • solve.estimate_t_ga: Estimates t_ga, the time offset between the gyroscope and the accelerometer.
  • solve.sigma_g: IMU noise from a still recording of this unit, measured density × 2.5. The other three sigmas are set the same way.

This is the config of the run on this page, with every path cut to its file name. Point the paths at your copy.

The solve took about 6 min on one desktop. It writes cam_imu.json, a calibration YAML and a report beside the config.

Dataset

Section titled “Dataset”
  • Hand-held recording 1 of 10, about 66 s. The run uses the whole recording.
  • Cameras: two 544 × 640 global-shutter mono cameras at 20 Hz and a 1920 × 1088 rolling-shutter colour camera at about 4.3 Hz, all fitted with the equidistant lens model. IMU at 400 Hz.
  • Board: AprilGrid 6×6, tag 55 mm, spacing 0.3, t36h11.
  • Access is by automatic approval on the Hub; the data is CC BY-NC 4.0, commercial use by request.
  • Solve window: 66.4 s.

Download: Calibench, looper-insight9/ → The recordings are listed there, not copied here.
