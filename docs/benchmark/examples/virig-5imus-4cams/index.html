Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

One MVIS camera + IMU run on recording 5imus_4cams_01: all four cameras and all five IMUs solved together.

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

Config and run

Section titled “Config and run”

The marked lines are the choices that matter for this rig:

  • input.imu_topics: Five IMU topics. The first is the base IMU; the other four are solved against it.
  • input.window_mode: MVIS picks the 80 s window with the most motion; this recording is shorter, so all of it is used.
  • camera_input.cameras: The three cameras after the first are added to the graph at 4 Hz, which keeps the solve small.
  • camera_input.path: The starting camera intrinsics, from camera-only solves of all 7 recordings.
  • solve.estimate_t_ga: Estimates t_ga, the time offset between the gyroscope and the accelerometer. The T265 IMUs need it most.
  • estimate.board_points: The printed board is not quite flat, so its shape is estimated too.
  • estimate.camera.intrinsics: Camera intrinsics stay free in the camera + IMU solve.

This is the config of the run on this page, with every path cut to its file name. Point the paths at your copy.

The solve took about 7 min on one desktop. It writes cam_imu.json, a calibration YAML and a report beside the config.

Dataset

Section titled “Dataset”
  • Recording 5imus_4cams_01, one of the 7 recordings of this rig. It is about 59 s long, shorter than the 80 s window MVIS asks for, so the whole recording is used.
  • Cameras: the fisheye pairs of two T265 units, all global shutter, with the equidistant lens model. Each pair is synced inside its unit, but the two units are not synced to each other; their time offsets are solved against the base IMU.
  • IMUs: the Xsens MTi is the base IMU; the two MicroStrain units and the two T265 IMUs are solved against it.
  • Board: AprilGrid 6×6, tag 88 mm, spacing 0.3, t36h11.
  • Solve window: 58.7 s.
