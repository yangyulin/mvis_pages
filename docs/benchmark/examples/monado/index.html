Monado: HP Reverb G2

Tracking-camera pair1 kHz IMUAprilGrid 6×6, 30 mmEuRoC mav0CC BY 4.0

What you'll see: One MVIS camera + IMU run on the HP Reverb G2 recording imucalib01 (round 1), and how close it lands to the calibration Monado ships with the data.

All recordings and tools: Results → HP Reverb G2 in the Sensor wiki → Downloads: Monado SLAM Datasets →

1. Data

Section titled “1. Data”
  • Recording MGC imucalib01, round 1, from the Monado SLAM Datasets (Collabora). It shows the board to the front camera pair, cam0 and cam1.
  • Cameras: 640 × 480 grayscale at 30 Hz, fitted with the equidistant lens model, as in the MVIS regression config for this headset. IMU at about 1 kHz.
  • Board: AprilGrid 6×6, tag 30 mm, spacing 0.3, t36h11. The tag is much smaller than the usual 88 mm, and board size scales every translation.
  • Solve window: 41.6 s.

Download: Monado SLAM Datasets → The recordings are listed there, not copied here.

2. Config

Section titled “2. Config”

The marked lines are the choices that matter for this rig:

  • board.tag_size: The 30 mm tag. A wrong tag size gives a calibration that converges cleanly and is uniformly wrong in scale.
  • input.window_mode: MVIS picks the 80 s window with the most motion.
  • camera_input.cameras: cam1 is added to the graph at 8 Hz, which keeps the solve small.
  • solve.estimate_t_ga: Estimates t_ga, the time offset between the gyroscope and the accelerometer.
  • estimate.camera.intrinsics: Camera intrinsics stay free in the camera + IMU solve.

This is the config of the run on this page, with every path cut to its file name. Point the paths at your copy.

3. Run

Section titled “3. Run”

The solve took about 3 min on one desktop. It writes cam_imu.json, a calibration YAML and a report beside the config.

4. Watch MVIS calibrate

Section titled “4. Watch MVIS calibrate”

MVIS's own player, from this run: the detected board corners, then the rig's path as the solve refines it. Press Play or drag the bar; pick a camera card to look through that camera. We publish no camera images.

Lever arms and rotations are each sensor in the base IMU frame; rotation is log(R) in rad. td is the time offset to the base IMU, t_imu = t_sensor + td. Reprojection is the median per camera (all cameras: median 0.130 px): how well the model fits the corners, not how accurate it is.

Δp is the distance between the two lever arms. Δθ = ‖log(RaT Rb)‖, the angle between the two rotations. A reference, not ground truth: it was solved by another tool on the same kind of data.

Monado ships a second, equidistant calibration of the same headset. Its rotations differ from this file's by about 4.5 mrad, so a few mrad is within how much the references themselves disagree.

The other two camera pairs Sensor wiki

The headset has four cameras, and each recording shows the board to one pair. The sensor page stitches all four together from three pair recordings. HP Reverb G2 →
