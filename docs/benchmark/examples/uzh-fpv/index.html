Demo and values

Section titled “Demo and values”

One MVIS camera + IMU run on the Snapdragon Flight's outdoor forward calibration recording, and one on the mDAVIS346's, each against the Kalibr calibration the dataset ships.

Reprojection median0.16 px
cam0–cam1 baseline79.3 mm
cam0 td−7.28 ms
cam0 lever arm23.3 mm

Reprojection is how far, in pixels, the detected corners lie from where the solve puts them. td is the camera–IMU time offset, and the lever arm is the camera–IMU position offset.

Calibration process

Section titled “Calibration process”

MVIS's own player, from this run: the detected board corners, then the rig's path as the solve refines it. Press Play or drag the bar; pick a camera card to look through that camera. We publish no camera images.

Config and run

Section titled “Config and run”

The marked lines are the choices that matter for this rig:

  • board: The UZH board: AprilGrid 5×4, tag 75 mm, spacing 0.2.
  • camera_input.path: Seed intrinsics: UZH's own camera-only Kalibr calibration, equidistant lens. MVIS refines them in this solve.
  • solve.sigma_g: IMU noise from the residuals of UZH's own Kalibr results, as UZH publishes no measured density. The other three sigmas are set the same way.
  • solve.estimate_t_ga: Estimates t_ga, the time offset between the gyroscope and the accelerometer.

This is the config of the run on this page, with every path cut to its file name. Point the paths at your copy.

The solve took about 3 min on one desktop. It writes cam_imu.json, a calibration YAML and a report beside the config.

Dataset

Section titled “Dataset”
  • Recording outdoor_forward_calib_snapdragon: the Snapdragon Flight's two 640 × 480 global-shutter cameras, fitted with the equidistant lens model as in UZH's own calibration, and its IMU at about 500 Hz.
  • IMU noise: UZH publishes no measured noise density, so the sigmas come from the residuals of UZH's own Kalibr results. They are estimates.
  • Board: AprilGrid 5×4, tag 75 mm, spacing 0.2, t36h11.
  • Cite Delmerico et al., "Are We Ready for Autonomous Drone Racing? The UZH-FPV Drone Racing Dataset", ICRA 2019. Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.
  • Solve window: 44.7 s.

Download: UZH-FPV datasets page → The recordings are listed there, not copied here.
