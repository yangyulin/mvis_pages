VI-Rig: 4 IMUs + 3 cameras

Our rig: a FLIR Blackfly and a RealSense T265 fisheye pair, all global shutter, with four IMUs from 100 to 500 Hz.

our data 3 global-shutter cameras4 IMUs, 100–500 Hz CC BY-NC 4.0

Batch 2026-11-10a · results commit fe8ee9f · Leaderboard · Methodology

Downloads → Dataset pageThe data → Hugging Face

Repeatability, not accuracy. There is no ground truth: a small spread shows that a tool repeats, not that it is right, and a difference shows that two tools disagree, not which one is wrong. The MVIS authors made every run, including Kalibr and Basalt.

T6 · camera + IMU

Section titled “T6 · camera + IMU”

3 GS cameras + 4 IMUs · 10 recordings

Read first: Kalibr's intrinsics and baselines are inputs from its own camera-only calibration; its td and lever are solved given them. Data settings differ between the tools.
