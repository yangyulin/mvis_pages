## Sensors for the MVIS datasets

<Image src={viRig} alt="VI-Rig with 4 IMUs and 5 cameras" width="320" />

**Visual-Inertial (VI)-Rig** — 4 IMUs and 5 cameras:

- MicroStrain GX3-25
- MicroStrain GX3-35
- Xsens MTI-100
- RealSense T265 IMU
- FLIR BlackFly
- RealSense T265 Left & Right cameras
- ELP Left & Right rolling-shutter cameras

### Topics and rates

- MicroStrain GX3-25 — 500 Hz, `/gx3_25/data`
- MicroStrain GX3-35 — 100 Hz, `/gx3_35/imu/data`
- Xsens MTI-100 — 400 Hz, `/imu/data`
- RealSense T265 IMU — 200 Hz, `/t265/imu`
- RealSense T265 Left — 30 Hz, `/t265/fisheye1/image_raw`, 848×800
- RealSense T265 Right — 30 Hz, `/t265/fisheye2/image_raw`, 848×800
- ELP Left rolling shutter — 25 Hz, `/elp/split_sync_image_node/left/image_raw`, 640×480
- ELP Right rolling shutter — 25 Hz, `/elp/split_sync_image_node/right/image_raw`, 640×480
