## The rig

Rates are measured from the bags. Shutter: every camera here is global shutter.

| Sensor | Type | Rate | Image size | ROS topic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FLIR Blackfly S BFS-U3-16S2M | camera, standard lens | 30 Hz | 720×540 (2×2 binned) | `/blackfly/image_raw` |
| RealSense T265, left | camera, fisheye (about 163°) | 30 Hz | 848×800 | `/t265/fisheye1/image_raw` |
| RealSense T265, right | camera, fisheye (about 163°) | 30 Hz | 848×800 | `/t265/fisheye2/image_raw` |
| Xsens MTi-100 | IMU | 400 Hz | – | `/imu/data` |
| MicroStrain 3DM-GX3-25 | IMU | 500 Hz | – | `/gx3_25/data` |
| MicroStrain 3DM-GX3-35 | IMU | 100 Hz | – | `/gx3_35/imu/data` |
| RealSense T265 IMU (BMI055) | IMU | 200 Hz | – | `/t265/imu` |

- **Board:** AprilGrid, 6×6 tags of family t36h11, tag size 88 mm, spacing 0.3 of the tag size. The target file
  [`april.yaml`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/blob/main/virig-4imus-3cams/april.yaml)
  is in the folder.
- **Board shape:** the printed board is not flat (about 2.4 mm rms). Our runs estimate its shape; see
  [Why calibrate the board shape](/why/board-shape/).
- **Drivers:** we recorded with these [ROS drivers](https://github.com/yangyulin/multi_sensor_drivers).
- **More on the T265:** [device page](/devices/intel-t265/).

## Recordings

- **Count:** 10 ROS1 bags, `virig_4imus_3cams_april_01.bag` to `virig_4imus_3cams_april_10.bag`.
- **Length:** 131 to 161 s each, 24 minutes in total.
- **Size:** 6.9 to 8.5 GB each, 76 GB in total.

### Time-offset sweep

The folder also holds [`tga-sweep/`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/virig-4imus-3cams/tga-sweep):
11 versions of recording 01, cut to a shorter window, with the T265 accelerometer delayed by a known amount (−50 to +50 ms). They test whether
a tool can find the delay between gyroscope and accelerometer ($t_{ga}$). Each copy is 3.3 GB. A README, the MVIS
calibration used for the correction and a `provenance.json` are beside them. See
[Why calibrate t_ga](/why/tga/).

## Download

Accept the terms on the [Hub page](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets) and set
`HF_TOKEN` to a read token from your Hugging Face settings (or run `hf auth login` once,
after `pip install huggingface_hub`). Then either:

```bash
# with MVIS: checks size and SHA-256, saves to $MVIS_DATASETS/mvis_4imus_3cams/
mvis datasets list --dataset mvis_4imus_3cams
mvis datasets download --select mvis_4imus_3cams/april_01   # one recording, 6.9 GB
mvis datasets download --dataset mvis_4imus_3cams           # all 10, 76 GB
```

```bash
# with the Hugging Face CLI: the 10 recordings and the board file, without the sweep
hf download calibench-vi/datasets --repo-type dataset \
  --include "virig-4imus-3cams/virig_*.bag" "virig-4imus-3cams/april.yaml" \
  --local-dir ./calibench
```

## Results

We ran MVIS and Kalibr on every recording, and Basalt on the camera-only runs (batch 2026-11-10a). The
[results page](/benchmark/results/virig-4imus-3cams/) shows every value and its spread.

| Run | Recordings solved |
|---|---|
| Camera only. Kalibr: the 3 cameras together. MVIS and Basalt: the Blackfly alone and the T265 pair, so no baseline to the Blackfly | MVIS 10 of 10 · Kalibr 10 of 10 · Basalt 10 of 10 |
| Camera + IMU, 3 cameras + 4 IMUs | MVIS 10 of 10 · Kalibr 10 of 10 · Basalt not run (one IMU per run) |

A few MVIS values from the camera + IMU runs. Each is the median over the 10 recordings, ± the standard deviation
between recordings. This shows how well MVIS repeats, not that it is right.

- **Blackfly focal length $f_x$:** 448.30 ± 0.61 px
- **Lever arm of the GX3-25 from the Xsens:** 131.31 ± 0.20 mm
- **Time offset of the T265 IMU to the Xsens:** 2.83 ± 0.21 ms
- **Reprojection error, median:** 0.35 px (Blackfly), 0.23 and 0.24 px (T265 pair)

The [worked example](/benchmark/examples/virig-4imus-3cams/) shows one full run with its config.

### Sensor order in the results files

The reference results in [`calibench-vi/results`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results) hold one
file per tool, batch and run (for example `MVIS/2026-11-10a/T6.json`) and the configs behind them in `configs/T6/`
(camera + IMU) and `configs/T6c/` (camera only). Kalibr and MVIS name the sensors in the same order. The base IMU is
the one the other sensors are referred to.

| Sensor | Blackfly | T265 left | T265 right | Xsens MTi-100 | GX3-25 | GX3-35 | T265 IMU |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kalibr | cam0 | cam1 | cam2 | imu0 | imu1 | imu2 | imu3 |
| MVIS | cam0 | cam1 | cam2 | base IMU | aux IMU 1 | aux IMU 2 | aux IMU 3 |

## Run it yourself

- **Start here:** the [worked example](/benchmark/examples/virig-4imus-3cams/) has the camera + IMU config we ran.
- **Steps:** a camera-only run first (`mvis cam -c <config>`), then camera + IMU (`mvis cam-imu -c <config>`). See
  [Camera-only calibration](/guides/camera-calibration/) and
  [Camera–IMU calibration](/guides/camera-imu-calibration/).
