Use this page to find the benchmark data, see what each dataset holds, and
cite it.

- **Where:** all benchmark data lives in the
  [**Calibench** Hugging Face organization](https://huggingface.co/calibench-vi).
- **Source of truth:** the
  [`calibench-vi/datasets`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets)
  repository.
- **Hosted:** our own VI-Rig captures (CC BY-NC 4.0).
- **Recipes:** download recipes for the third-party datasets the benchmark
  evaluates against.
- **Old links:** the Google Drive links from the legacy site are retired.

## Hosted — VI-Rig captures

We collected these on a self-built VI-Rig for research on multi-visual-inertial
calibration. Reference Kalibr and MVIS results for these captures are in
[`calibench-vi/results`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results).

Terms in the table:

- **IMU:** inertial measurement unit (a gyroscope and an accelerometer)
- **GS / global shutter:** the camera exposes the whole image at once
- **Rolling shutter:** the camera reads the image out row by row
- **AprilGrid:** a printed grid of AprilTag markers (square codes the software
  finds in each image)
- **ArUco:** another family of square marker tags

| Dataset | Sensors | Sequences | Data |
|---|---|---|---|
| [4 IMUs + 3 cameras](/datasets/virig-4imus-3cams/) | 4 IMUs, 3 global-shutter | 10 AprilGrid | [`virig-4imus-3cams`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/virig-4imus-3cams) |
| 4 IMUs + 3 cameras — ArUco | same rig, ArUco-tag sequences | 17 | [`virig-4imus-3cams-aruco`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/virig-4imus-3cams-aruco) |
| [4 IMUs + 4 cameras](/datasets/virig-4imus-4cams/) | 4 IMUs, 2 GS + 2 rolling shutter | 15 AprilGrid (+7 raw) | [`virig-4imus-4cams`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/virig-4imus-4cams) |
| 5 IMUs + 4 cameras | 5 IMUs (two T265 stereo fisheye pairs), 4 cameras | 7 AprilGrid | [`virig-5imus-4cams`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/virig-5imus-4cams) |

## Third-party datasets (recipes)

Calibench hosts only data we own. Every third-party dataset the benchmark
evaluates ships as a `download.py` recipe (manifest + checksums). The recipe
pulls the data from its original host. The recipes cover:

- EuRoC MAV
- TUM VI
- TUM RGB-D
- TUM VIE
- TUM Double-Sphere
- TUM RS
- Monado MSD
- Hilti 2022
- Hilti 2023 Handheld
- PennCOSYVIO
- an urban fisheye set

For the exact list and licenses, see
[`recipes/`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/recipes)
and its
[`SOURCES.md`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/blob/main/recipes/SOURCES.md).
The [leaderboard](/benchmark/results/) evaluates calibration methods across
these datasets plus the hosted VI-Rig captures.

These datasets have complete MVIS runs, documented as worked examples on this site:

| Dataset | Rig | Example |
|---|---|---|
| TUM VI | Stereo 1024² fisheye + BMI160 | [TUM VI](/benchmark/examples/tum-vi/) |
| Monado SLAM (HP Reverb G2) | Tracking-camera pair + 1 kHz IMU | [Monado](/benchmark/examples/monado/) |
| VI-Rig 4 IMUs + 3 cams | 3 global-shutter cameras + 4 IMUs | [VI-Rig](/benchmark/examples/virig-4imus-3cams/) |
| UZH-FPV | Snapdragon Flight stereo + IMU; mDAVIS346 + IMU | [UZH-FPV](/benchmark/examples/uzh-fpv/) |
| Looper Insight 9 | 3 cameras (one rolling shutter) + 400 Hz IMU | [Looper Insight 9](/benchmark/examples/looper-insight9/) |

To contribute one, see [Submit a dataset](/contribute/submit-dataset/).

## VI-Rig sensors

We use a self-built VI-Rig to collect data. The rig has 4 IMUs, one monocular
camera and 2 stereo pairs. Counting each stereo camera as two monocular cameras,
we have 5 cameras total.

<Image src={viRig} alt="VI-Rig with 4 IMUs and 5 cameras" width="320" />

**Visual-Inertial (VI)-Rig** — 4 IMUs and 5 cameras:

- MicroStrain GX3-25
- MicroStrain GX3-35
- Xsens MTI-100
- RealSense T265 IMU
- FLIR BlackFly
- RealSense T265 Left & Right cameras
- ELP Left & Right rolling-shutter cameras

The ROS drivers can be found [here](https://github.com/yangyulin/multi_sensor_drivers).
