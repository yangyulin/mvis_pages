## Degeneracy

This page will explain which motions leave parts of the calibration unsolved.
It is coming soon.

We call such a motion degenerate: under it, some calibration parameters cannot be recovered.
The planned analysis covers degenerate motion patterns such as:

- one-axis rotation
- planar translation
- constant velocity
