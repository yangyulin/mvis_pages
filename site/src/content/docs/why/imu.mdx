Use this page to see what an IMU calibration buys a tracker downstream.
Data: 24 headset recordings from the [Monado SLAM Datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets/collabora/monado-slam-datasets); the VI-Rig numbers are in [the last section](#4-on-vi-rig-the-t265-accel-scale-is-off-on-every-axis).

**The answer first:**

- **Mono gains most:** correcting the IMU and timing with MVIS cuts the median OpenVINS mono SE3 ATE from 28.80 to
  9.56 mm, by two thirds.
- **Stereo gains less:** the median stereo SE3 ATE drops from 8.94 to 7.04 mm, and the 1 s drift improves on 24/24
  recordings.
- **Online calibration does not replace it:** on the as-published recordings, only stereo with online $t_d$ ties
  the corrected recording; every other setting stays behind.
- **Online calibration on top adds nothing:** on the corrected recordings, keep it off.
- **Same kind of error on VI-Rig:** the T265 accel scale is off by 1.7–2.0 % on every axis.

How to turn it on: estimate the IMU intrinsics in [Camera–IMU calibration](/guides/camera-imu-calibration/#3-turn-on-imu-intrinsics), then write a corrected recording with [`mvis apply-calib`](/guides/roundtrip/#export-a-corrected-dataset).

## The setup

We ran the OpenVINS visual-inertial tracker on each recording twice: once on the recording as published and once on
the recording corrected by MVIS.

**Recordings:** 24 `imucalib` recordings from the Monado SLAM Datasets:

| Headset | Recordings |
|---|---|
| [Valve Index](/devices/valve-index/) | 8 |
| [HP Reverb G2](/devices/hp-reverb-g2/) | 12 |
| [Samsung Odyssey+](/devices/samsung-odyssey-plus/) | 4 |

**Two arms:** the two runs of a recording differ only in the input recording.

- **As published:** the recording as the dataset ships it.
- **Corrected:** the same recording after `mvis apply-calib` with the MVIS calibration. It applies:
  - the **IMU intrinsics**, the gyro and accel scale and misalignment $\mathbf{D}_w$ and $\mathbf{D}_a$;
  - removal of the **static bias**;
  - the camera–IMU time offset $t_d$ of each camera, by shifting the image stamps;
  - the gyro–accel time offset $t_{ga}$, by re-interpolating the accelerometer.
- **Images:** unchanged.

**Shared by both arms:**

- the same MVIS camera intrinsics (`kb4`) and camera–IMU extrinsics;
- identical OpenVINS configs, byte for byte.

So the test measures one thing: applying the MVIS IMU and time correction, given the MVIS geometry.

**Ground truth:** the lighthouse poses that come with the dataset.

**Metrics:**

- **SE3 ATE** (mm): position error after a rigid alignment to the ground truth. This is the primary metric.
- **RPE 1 s** (mm): position drift over 1 s windows.
- **Scale error** (%): $\lvert s - 1 \rvert$, where $s$ is the Sim3 scale. It shows how far the trajectory size is
  off after a similarity alignment.
- **Medians and wins:** each number below is a median over recordings. "Better" counts the recordings where the
  corrected recording wins.
- **p:** Wilcoxon signed-rank p, paired by recording, not corrected for multiple tests.

## 1. Correcting the IMU cuts mono position error by two thirds

Here OpenVINS estimates no calibration while it runs. Lower is better.

| Metric | As published | Corrected | Corrected better | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stereo SE3 ATE (mm) | 8.94 | 7.04 | 20/24 | $9.1\times10^{-5}$ |
| Stereo RPE 1 s (mm) | 5.51 | 4.23 | 24/24 | $1.2\times10^{-7}$ |
| Mono SE3 ATE (mm) | 28.80 | 9.56 | 21/23 | $2.1\times10^{-4}$ |
| Mono RPE 1 s (mm) | 9.95 | 6.79 | 21/23 | $2.1\times10^{-4}$ |
| Mono scale error (%) | 5.81 | 1.90 | 19/23 | $3.3\times10^{-5}$ |
| Stereo scale error (%) | 1.40 | 1.15 | 11/24 | 0.88 (not significant) |

- **Mono has 23 pairs:** one Valve recording never starts in mono on either recording.
- **Mono gains most:** one camera cannot see metric scale, so the tracker leans on the IMU. A wrong IMU likely shows
  up directly.
- **Stereo gains less:** the stereo baseline gives scale, which likely hides part of the IMU error.

Per headset, SE3 ATE in mm:

| Headset | Stereo | Mono |
|---|---|---|
| Valve Index (8 stereo, 7 mono) | 4.13 → 2.73 | 12.15 → 7.55 |
| HP Reverb G2 (12) | 9.91 → 7.39 | 29.08 → 9.49 |
| Samsung Odyssey+ (4) | 21.29 → 9.96 | 47.51 → 13.67 |

- **Mono failures as published:** five as-published mono runs lost scale or diverged.
- **Mono failures corrected:** with the correction, only one failed: MIC10, a Valve recording that fails on both
  recordings.

## 2. Online calibration on the published recordings does not catch up

OpenVINS can estimate some calibration while it runs: one $t_d$ for all cameras, the IMU intrinsics and the
camera–IMU extrinsics. Here the corrected recording with nothing online meets the as-published recording with
online estimation on. SE3 ATE in mm, medians:

| Cameras | Online on the as-published recording | Corrected, nothing online | As published + online |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stereo | $t_d$ | 7.04 | 6.80 |
| Stereo | IMU intrinsics | 7.04 | 10.74 |
| Stereo | IMU intrinsics + $t_d$ | 7.04 | 8.19 |
| Stereo | IMU intrinsics + $t_d$ + extrinsics | 7.04 | 11.62 |
| Mono | $t_d$ | 9.56 | 18.06 |
| Mono | IMU intrinsics | 9.56 | 25.50 |
| Mono | IMU intrinsics + $t_d$ | 9.56 | 14.57 |

- **Stereo + online $t_d$ is a tie:** corrected better in 14/24, p = 0.2.
- **Every other setting** stays behind the corrected recording.
- **Mono** stays behind at every setting, each in 21/23 recordings.
- **$t_{ga}$:** OpenVINS has no $t_{ga}$ parameter, so it cannot model this offset at all. See
  [Why calibrate t_ga](/why/tga/).

## 3. Online calibration on corrected recordings adds nothing

Turning online calibration on for the corrected recording does not help:

- **Stereo + $t_d$:** 7.04 → 7.33 mm. Online is better in only 7/24.
- **No setting** lowers SE3 ATE significantly. So use the corrected recording with online calibration off.

The online values still check MVIS:

- **Independent start:** the online values start at $t_d = 0$, so the agreement does not lean on MVIS.
- **Agreement:** camera $t_d$ in ms, as-published recording, stereo + online $t_d$:

| Headset | MVIS $t_d$ (cam0) | OpenVINS online $t_d$ |
|---|---|---|
| Valve Index | −0.19 | −0.22 |
| HP Reverb G2 | −1.33 | −1.12 |
| Samsung Odyssey+ | −1.44 | −1.21 |

- **Corrected recording:** the online $t_d$ stays near 0, as it should once the offset is removed.

## 4. On VI-Rig, the T265 accel scale is off on every axis

The VI-Rig dataset [virig-4imus-4cams](/reference/datasets/virig-4imus-4cams/) shows why this matters on a real
rig. We take the 15-recording [results](/benchmark/results/virig-4imus-4cams/) of family T8:

- **Rig:** ELP stereo, T265 IMU as base, Xsens as second IMU.
- **Model:** MVIS with Kalibr's IMU model (`kalibr`).

Accel scales, the diagonal of $\mathbf{D}_a$:

| IMU | $\mathbf{D}_a$ x | $\mathbf{D}_a$ y | $\mathbf{D}_a$ z |
|---|---|---|---|
| T265 IMU | 1.01989 | 1.01905 | 1.01668 |
| Xsens MTi | 0.99997 | 0.99932 | 1.00093 |

- **T265 IMU:** its accel scale is off by 1.7–2.0 % on every axis.
- **Xsens:** its scale stays within 0.1 % of 1. It is a check on the T265 result.
- **Same kind of fix:** the corrected Monado recordings apply the MVIS IMU intrinsics $\mathbf{D}_a$ and
  $\mathbf{D}_w$ in the same way.

To do it on your rig:

- **Estimate** the IMU intrinsics in [step 4, Camera–IMU calibration](/guides/camera-imu-calibration/).
- **Write** a corrected recording with `mvis apply-calib` in [step 8, Round-trip validation](/guides/roundtrip/#export-a-corrected-dataset).

**Conclusion:**

- **Correct the IMU before the tracker:** the MVIS IMU and time correction cuts mono SE3 ATE by two thirds and helps
  stereo too.
- **Online calibration is no substitute:** it does not catch up on the as-published recordings, and adds nothing on
  the corrected ones.
- **Keep it off:** run the corrected recording with online calibration off.

## Limits

<div class="not-content rp-honest">
  <ul>
    <li><b>In-sample:</b> MVIS was solved on the same recordings. A check on held-out recordings is still open.</li>
    <li><b>Shared geometry:</b> Both arms use the MVIS camera intrinsics and extrinsics. This is not MVIS against
      the best calibration for the as-published data.</li>
    <li><b>"As published" is not raw:</b> The dataset already carries its own Basalt IMU correction.</li>
    <li><b>Valve corrected twice:</b> On Valve that correction was applied twice, which leaves an accel scale error
      of about 6 %. Part of the Valve mono gain likely undoes that.</li>
    <li><b>One combined gain:</b> The correction fixes IMU intrinsics, bias, td and t_ga together. Their effects
      were not separated.</li>
    <li><b>Too-large trajectories:</b> Reverb G2 and Odyssey+ trajectories come out about 1–2 % too large in both
      arms. The cause is not known.</li>
    <li><b>One recording gets worse:</b> Odyssey+ MOC11 stereo goes from 11.4 to 16.6 mm with the correction,
      though its RPE and mono improve.</li>
    <li><b>Small group:</b> Odyssey+ has only 4 pairs.</li>
    <li><b>Priors not tuned:</b> The OpenVINS online priors are fixed and were not tuned.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
