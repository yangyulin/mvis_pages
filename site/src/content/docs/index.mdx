## Multi-Visual-Inertial Sensor Calibration

Open MVIS is an open-source toolbox and public benchmark for the spatial-temporal
and intrinsic calibration of multi-IMU + multi-camera rigs. Our reference rig
(pictured above) carries **4 IMUs and 5 cameras**:

- MicroStrain GX3-25
- MicroStrain GX3-35
- Xsens MTI-100
- RealSense T265 IMU
- FLIR BlackFly
- RealSense T265 Left & Right cameras
- ELP Left & Right rolling-shutter cameras

## Main features

This is designed to support spatial-temporal and intrinsic calibration for
multiple IMUs, gyros, and cameras. The main features:

- IMU intrinsic calibration (Kalibr and RPNG models)
- Gyroscope intrinsic calibration (Kalibr and RPNG models)
- Camera intrinsic calibration / refinement (rad-tan and equidistant models)
- IMU–IMU spatial-temporal calibration (rotation, translation, time offset)
- IMU–gyro spatial-temporal calibration (rotation, time offset)
- IMU–camera spatial-temporal calibration (rotation, translation, time offset)
- Rolling-shutter readout-time calibration (first / middle / last row image timestamp)
- Evaluation scripts for calibration results
- Open-sourced MVIS datasets
- Both [Kalibr AprilGrid](https://github.com/ethz-asl/kalibr/wiki/calibration-targets)
  and [ArUco](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d5/dae/tutorial_aruco_detection.html) targets
