Hosted in the Calibench Hugging Face organization:
[`calibench-vi/datasets` → `virig-4imus-3cams`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/virig-4imus-3cams)
(CC-BY-4.0). A companion set of 17 ArUco-tag sequences on the same rig is in
[`virig-4imus-3cams-aruco`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/virig-4imus-3cams-aruco).

## 4 IMUs + 3 Cams

| Sensor | Hz | Topic | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| MicroStrain GX3-25 | 500 | `/gx3_25/data` | — |
| MicroStrain GX3-35 | 100 | `/gx3_35/imu/data` | — |
| Xsens MTI-100 | 400 | `/imu/data` | — |
| RealSense T265 IMU | 200 | `/t265/imu` | — |
| FLIR BlackFly | 30 | `/blackfly/image_raw` | 720×540 |
| RealSense T265 Left | 30 | `/t265/fisheye1/image_raw` | 848×800 |
| RealSense T265 Right | 30 | `/t265/fisheye2/image_raw` | 848×800 |

## Reference results

Per-config Kalibr and MVIS reference results (dated snapshots, JSON against the
benchmark schema) are published in
[`calibench-vi/results`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results) —
see the
[`Kalibr/`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results/tree/main/Kalibr)
and
[`MVIS/`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results/tree/main/MVIS)
trees (`virig_*` and `mvis_*` configs) — and are ranked on the
[leaderboard](/benchmark/leaderboard/).

### Sensor numbering for Kalibr and MVIS results

| Sensor | BlackFly | T265 Left | T265 Right | GX3-25 | GX3-35 | Xsens MTI-100 | T265 IMU |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kalibr | cam0 | cam1 | cam2 | imu0 | imu1 | imu2 | imu3 |
| MVIS | Cam2 | Cam0 | Cam1 | Ib | Is0 | Is1 | Is2 |

## Running MVIS

```bash
mvis calibrate -c virig_3cams_cam.yaml   # cameras first
mvis cam-imu   -c virig_3cams_cam_imu.yaml
```
