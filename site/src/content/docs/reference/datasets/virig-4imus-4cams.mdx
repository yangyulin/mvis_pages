## 4 IMUs + 4 Cams

| Sensor | Hz | Topic | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| MicroStrain GX3-25 | 500 | `/gx3_25/data` | — |
| MicroStrain GX3-35 | 100 | `/gx3_35/imu/data` | — |
| Xsens MTI-100 | 400 | `/imu/data` | — |
| RealSense T265 IMU | 200 | `/t265/imu` | — |
| RealSense T265 Left | 30 | `/t265/fisheye1/image_raw` | 848×800 |
| RealSense T265 Right | 30 | `/t265/fisheye2/image_raw` | 848×800 |
| ELP Left rolling shutter | 25 | `/elp/split_sync_image_node/left/image_raw` | 640×480 |
| ELP Right rolling shutter | 25 | `/elp/split_sync_image_node/right/image_raw` | 640×480 |

## Reference results

Per-config Kalibr and MVIS reference results (dated snapshots, JSON against the
benchmark schema) are published in
[`calibench-vi/results`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results) —
see the
[`Kalibr/`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results/tree/main/Kalibr)
and
[`MVIS/`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results/tree/main/MVIS)
trees (`mvis_t265*` and `mvis_elp*` configs) — and are ranked on the
[leaderboard](/benchmark/leaderboard/).

When calibrating IMU and ELP cameras with Kalibr, the
[Kalibr extension](https://github.com/JzHuai0108/kalibr.git) is used.

### Sensor numbering for Kalibr and MVIS results

| Sensor | T265 Left | T265 Right | ELP Left | ELP Right | GX3-25 | GX3-35 | Xsens MTI-100 | T265 IMU |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kalibr with T265 | cam0 | cam1 | — | — | imu0 | imu1 | imu2 | imu3 |
| Kalibr with ELP | — | — | cam0 | cam1 | imu0 | imu1 | imu2 | imu3 |
| MVIS | Cam0 | Cam1 | Cam2 | Cam3 | Ib | Is0 | Is1 | Is2 |

## Running MVIS

```bash
mvis calibrate -c virig_4cams_cam.yaml   # cameras first
mvis cam-imu   -c virig_4cams_cam_imu.yaml
```
