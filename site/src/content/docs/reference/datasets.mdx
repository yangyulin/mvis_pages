Datasets released with Open MVIS, plus the public datasets used as worked
examples.

## Released — VI-Rig

Collected on a self-built VI-Rig for research on multi-visual-inertial
calibration. Both releases include Kalibr and MVIS reference results.

| Dataset | Sensors | Sequences |
|---|---|---|
| [4 IMUs + 3 cameras](/reference/datasets/virig-4imus-3cams/) | 4 IMUs, 3 global-shutter | 10 AprilGrid + 1 static |
| [4 IMUs + 4 cameras](/reference/datasets/virig-4imus-4cams/) | 4 IMUs, 2 GS + 2 rolling shutter | 15 |

## VI-Rig sensors

We use a self-built VI-Rig to collect data. The rig contains 4 IMUs, one monocular camera, and 2 stereo pairs. Treating each stereo camera as two monocular cameras, we have 5 cameras total.

<Image src={viRig} alt="VI-Rig with 4 IMUs and 5 cameras" width="320" />

**Visual-Inertial (VI)-Rig** — 4 IMUs and 5 cameras:

- MicroStrain GX3-25
- MicroStrain GX3-35
- Xsens MTI-100
- RealSense T265 IMU
- FLIR BlackFly
- RealSense T265 Left & Right cameras
- ELP Left & Right rolling-shutter cameras

ROS drivers can be found [here](https://github.com/yangyulin/multi_sensor_drivers).
