All benchmark data lives in the
[**Calibench** Hugging Face organization](https://huggingface.co/calibench-vi).
The [`calibench-vi/datasets`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets)
repository is the source of truth: it hosts our own VI-Rig captures
(CC-BY-4.0) and ships download recipes for the third-party datasets the
benchmark evaluates against. The Google Drive links from the legacy site are
retired.

## Hosted — VI-Rig captures

Collected on a self-built VI-Rig for research on multi-visual-inertial
calibration. Reference Kalibr and MVIS results for these captures are published
in [`calibench-vi/results`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results).

| Dataset | Sensors | Sequences | Data |
|---|---|---|---|
| [4 IMUs + 3 cameras](/reference/datasets/virig-4imus-3cams/) | 4 IMUs, 3 global-shutter | 10 AprilGrid | [`virig-4imus-3cams`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/virig-4imus-3cams) |
| 4 IMUs + 3 cameras — ArUco | same rig, ArUco-tag sequences | 17 | [`virig-4imus-3cams-aruco`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/virig-4imus-3cams-aruco) |
| [4 IMUs + 4 cameras](/reference/datasets/virig-4imus-4cams/) | 4 IMUs, 2 GS + 2 rolling shutter | 15 AprilGrid (+7 raw) | [`virig-4imus-4cams`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/virig-4imus-4cams) |
| 5 IMUs + 4 cameras | 5 IMUs (two T265 stereo fisheye pairs), 4 cameras | 7 AprilGrid | [`virig-5imus-4cams`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/virig-5imus-4cams) |

## Third-party datasets (recipes)

Calibench hosts only data we own; every third-party dataset the benchmark
evaluates ships as a `download.py` recipe (manifest + checksums) that pulls it
from its original host — EuRoC MAV, TUM VI, TUM RGB-D, TUM VIE, TUM
Double-Sphere, TUM RS, Monado MSD, Hilti 2022, Hilti 2023 Handheld,
PennCOSYVIO, and an urban fisheye set. See
[`recipes/`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/recipes)
and its
[`SOURCES.md`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/blob/main/recipes/SOURCES.md)
for the exact list and licenses. The [leaderboard](/benchmark/leaderboard/)
evaluates calibration methods across these datasets plus the hosted VI-Rig
captures.

Datasets with complete MVIS runs documented as worked examples on this site:

| Dataset | Rig | Example |
|---|---|---|
| TUM VI | Stereo 1024² fisheye + BMI160 | [TUM VI](/examples/tum-vi/) |
| TUM MIC (Monado / Valve Index) | Stereo fisheye + IMU | [Monado](/examples/monado/) |
| looperrobotics SA16 | 2–3 cameras + 400 Hz IMU | [Looper SA16](/examples/looper-sa16/) |

Contributing one: [Submit a dataset](/contribute/submit-dataset/).

## VI-Rig sensors

We use a self-built VI-Rig to collect data. The rig contains 4 IMUs, one monocular camera, and 2 stereo pairs. Treating each stereo camera as two monocular cameras, we have 5 cameras total.

<Image src={viRig} alt="VI-Rig with 4 IMUs and 5 cameras" width="320" />

**Visual-Inertial (VI)-Rig** — 4 IMUs and 5 cameras:

- MicroStrain GX3-25
- MicroStrain GX3-35
- Xsens MTI-100
- RealSense T265 IMU
- FLIR BlackFly
- RealSense T265 Left & Right cameras
- ELP Left & Right rolling-shutter cameras

ROS drivers can be found [here](https://github.com/yangyulin/multi_sensor_drivers).
