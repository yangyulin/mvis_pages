What the [leaderboard](/benchmark/leaderboard/) numbers mean and how they are
produced. The metric definitions are shared with
[Evaluation & metrics](/reference/evaluation/), so a local run and a published
entry measure the same thing.

## What the board ranks

There is one leaderboard per dataset. MVIS, Kalibr and Basalt each calibrate the same recordings of a rig, and the
board ranks them on **repeatability**: how little each parameter varies across recordings. The parameters are `fx`,
`fy`, `cx`, `cy`, the stereo baseline, the camera or IMU lever arm, the time offset `td` and the rolling-shutter
readout `tr`.

:::caution[Repeatability is not accuracy]
These datasets have no metrological ground truth. A method that repeats the same bias on every recording scores well.
MVIS checks accuracy separately, against known truth, in its digital-twin regression
([Regression testing](/contribute/regression-testing/)).
:::

## Scoring

For each parameter, each method's score is its standard deviation across recordings divided by the smallest among the
methods, capped at 10.

| Level | Score |
|---|---|
| Family | Mean over the parameters that apply to the method |
| Dataset | Mean over all the dataset's families |

An aggregate like this encodes a judgement about what matters, so the per-family tables are always shown beneath it.

## Missing values

| Case | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Parameter the tool holds fixed by design, such as Kalibr's intrinsics in its camera + IMU solve | Left out for that tool |
| Parameter the tool should solve but has no result for, including fewer than 2 recordings | Scores 10 |
| Family the tool cannot run | Shown as `10 (not run)`, and counts in the dataset score |
| Family it ran with nothing scorable | Shown as `n/a`, and left out of the dataset score |

So a tool is penalised on a dataset for families it cannot run, such as Basalt on the multi-IMU rigs, or Kalibr and Basalt
on the rolling-shutter cameras of T8. The penalty reflects what the tool supports, not a failed solve.

## Known limits

- MVIS rows and the Kalibr and Basalt runs were not all made on the same date, or with the same data settings on T6, T7 and T8.
- A method's score moves when another method improves, because scores are relative to the best.

## Versioning

Each batch is dated and kept in the history of
[`calibench-vi/results`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results), so a comparison can be cited as
"Open MVIS leaderboard, batch 2026-11-02, accessed &lt;date&gt;".
