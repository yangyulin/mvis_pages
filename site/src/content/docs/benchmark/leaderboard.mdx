The calibration results of MVIS, [Kalibr](https://github.com/ethz-asl/kalibr) and
[Basalt](https://gitlab.com/VladyslavUsenko/basalt) on the same datasets: every parameter as median ± sd (n) across
recordings, with the differences between tools. This is batch **2026-11-04a**. The records live in
[`calibench-vi/results`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results), and the
[Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/calibench-vi/leaderboard) shows the same tables.

**There is no ground truth.** Differences show where tools disagree, not who is right. Spread shows repeatability
across recordings, not accuracy. The MVIS authors run this board and made every run, including Kalibr and Basalt.

Kalibr on T7 was re-run on 2026-11-02 on the corrected bags; T1 bag 09's were run on 2026-11-04; the other Kalibr and Basalt runs are from 2026-10-22 or earlier. Data settings still differ from MVIS on T6, T7 and T8, so read those three with care. More in
[Methodology](/benchmark/methodology/).

## Family numbers

Each family is one test in the MVIS regression suite. The numbers changed on 2026-11-02 so the VI-Rig datasets come
first, and older material uses the old ones. New (old): T1 (T3), T2 (T4), T3 (T1), T4 (T2), T5 (T5), T6 (T11), T7 (T8),
T8 (T12), T9 (T6), T10 (T7), T11 (T10), T12 (T9).
