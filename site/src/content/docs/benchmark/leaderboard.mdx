Calibration methods ranked across sensor configurations and algorithm families.
Entries are evaluated on the
[Calibench datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets) —
our hosted VI-Rig captures plus recipe-fetched third-party sets (see
[Datasets](/reference/datasets/)) — and the ranking data itself lives in
[`calibench-vi/results`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results).
Each entry reports reprojection error, extrinsic accuracy against the Open MVIS
consensus baseline, time-offset error, runtime, and convergence rate — with
mean ± stddev over N runs where the submitter provided them.

<LeaderboardTable />

Filter state is preserved in the URL, so a specific comparison can be cited
directly — for example
`/benchmark/leaderboard/?config=multi_imu_multi_cam&sort=timeoffset_err_ms`.

## Reading the table

- **Lower is better** for every error metric; **higher is better** for
  convergence rate.
- **`—` means not reported**, not zero. Absence is shown rather than filled in.
- **Compare within a sensor configuration**, not across. A single-IMU
  single-camera result and a four-IMU result are different problems.

How the categories are defined, how scores are computed, and what the baseline
is: [Methodology](/benchmark/methodology/).
