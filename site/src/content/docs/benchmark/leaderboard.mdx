MVIS, [Kalibr](https://github.com/ethz-asl/kalibr) and
[Basalt](https://gitlab.com/VladyslavUsenko/basalt) calibrated on the same recordings, with one
leaderboard per dataset. This is batch **2026-11-02**. The records live in
[`calibench-vi/results`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/results), and there is an
[interactive version](https://huggingface.co/spaces/calibench-vi/leaderboard) with every parameter.

## How to read a score

- **The score measures repeatability, not accuracy.** It compares how much each parameter varies across
  recordings of the same rig. These datasets have no ground truth, so a method can repeat well and still be biased.
- **Lower is better, and 1.00 is the best.** A score of 2 means twice the spread of the best method on that
  parameter, averaged over the parameters. Scores are capped at 10.
- **`10 (not run)` means the tool cannot run that family**, and the dataset score counts it. `n/a` means it ran but
  nothing it estimates could be scored there, so that family is left out.
- **Compare within a dataset**, not across datasets.

Kalibr and Basalt are the runs of 2026-10-22. The MVIS rows use later settings, including different data settings
on T6, T7 and T8, so read those three with care. Full rules: [Methodology](/benchmark/methodology/).

## Family numbers

Each family is one test in the MVIS regression suite. The numbers changed on 2026-11-02 so the VI-Rig datasets come
first, and older material uses the old ones. New (old): T1 (T3), T2 (T4), T3 (T1), T4 (T2), T5 (T5), T6 (T11), T7 (T8),
T8 (T12), T9 (T6), T10 (T7), T11 (T10), T12 (T9).
