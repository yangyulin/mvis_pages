This page compares MVIS, [Kalibr](https://github.com/ethz-asl/kalibr) and [Basalt](https://gitlab.com/VladyslavUsenko/basalt)
on all 8 camera-IMU calibration bags of the [UZH-FPV Drone Racing dataset](https://fpv.ifi.uzh.ch/datasets/).
We show the results in the same table as the [leaderboard](/benchmark/results/).

- **Devices:** two, each recorded indoors and outdoors.
- **Camera angle:** 45° and facing forward.
- **Scope:** a one-off study, not part of the leaderboard or the regression suite.

**There is no ground truth.** Differences show where tools disagree, not who is right. The MVIS authors made every
run, including Kalibr and Basalt.

## IMU weights

**All three tools use IMU sigmas taken from the residuals in UZH's own Kalibr results, not the MVIS defaults.**
Sigmas are the noise levels that set how much each IMU measurement counts in the fit.
UZH publishes no measured IMU noise. The sigmas in its `imu.yaml` are the same on all 8 bags (gyro 0.05, accel 0.1).

For each bag we derive the sigmas in two steps:

1. Take the RMS of Kalibr's 3-axis residual norm.
2. Divide it by √(3 × IMU rate).

| | Gyro | Accel |
|---|---|---|
| From the residuals | 3.0–8.5e-4 rad/s/√Hz | 1.1–5.2e-3 m/s²/√Hz |
| MVIS defaults | 1e-2 | 2e-2 |

Bias sigmas keep the MVIS default ratio of 0.1. These are estimates, not measured noise.

**The choice matters most on the Davis.** The table shows the difference between the indoor and outdoor MVIS
rotation on each device's forward bags. Snapdragon moves less, and its forward bags get slightly worse.

| Device | With these weights | With the defaults |
|---|---|---|
| Davis | 4.1 mrad | 38.9 mrad |
| Snapdragon | 4.2 mrad | 2.2 mrad |

## Reading these results

The lever (lever arm) is the camera–IMU position offset. The time offset (td) is the camera–IMU time offset.

- **One Basalt solve is left out.** On `davis_indoor_forward` with these weights, Basalt gives a 120 mm lever and an
  accel scale (accelerometer scale factor) of 0.84. So its Davis column has 3 of 4 bags. At Basalt's default noise that bag is plausible, but
  `snapdragon_indoor_45` then fails with a broken spline.
- **Basalt's Davis distance stands out.** Its camera-to-IMU distance is 33.6 mm. Kalibr gives 23.2 mm and MVIS
  22.8 mm on the same 3 bags. We haven't established why. At default noise Basalt's lever z is 14–25 mm.
- **Each device's 4 bags form one family**, because the camera and IMU are on the same device. That assumes the
  camera-IMU geometry stayed the same across recordings, which we can't confirm.
- **Snapdragon's time offset really does differ between bags**, with an sd (standard deviation) near 4.7 ms in every tool. So a small
  difference between tools on that row says little about each bag.
- **Rotations aren't in this table**, the same as on the leaderboard. The IMU models define the IMU frame differently.
