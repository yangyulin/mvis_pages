The [UZH-FPV Drone Racing dataset](https://fpv.ifi.uzh.ch/datasets/) ships camera-IMU calibration bags for two
devices, recorded indoors and outdoors with the camera at 45° and facing forward. We ran
MVIS, [Kalibr](https://github.com/ethz-asl/kalibr) and [Basalt](https://gitlab.com/VladyslavUsenko/basalt) on all
8 bags and show them in the same table as the [leaderboard](/benchmark/results/). This is a one-off study, not
part of the leaderboard or the regression suite.

**There is no ground truth.** Differences show where tools disagree, not who is right. The MVIS authors made every
run, including Kalibr and Basalt.

## IMU weights

**All three tools use IMU sigmas taken from the residuals in UZH's own Kalibr results, not the MVIS defaults.** UZH
publishes no measured IMU noise, and the sigmas in its `imu.yaml` are the same on all 8 bags (gyro 0.05, accel 0.1).
For each bag we take the RMS of Kalibr's 3-axis residual norm and divide by √(3 × IMU rate). That gives gyro
3.0–8.5e-4 rad/s/√Hz and accel 1.1–5.2e-3 m/s²/√Hz, against the MVIS defaults of 1e-2 and 2e-2. Bias sigmas keep the
MVIS default ratio of 0.1. These are estimates, not measured noise.

The choice matters most on the Davis. On its forward bags, the difference between the indoor and outdoor MVIS rotation
is 4.1 mrad with these weights and 38.9 mrad with the defaults. Snapdragon moves less, and its forward bags get
slightly worse, from 2.2 to 4.2 mrad.

## The rig

| Device | Cameras | IMU | Bags |
|---|---|---|---|
| mDAVIS346 | 1 global-shutter, 346×260, equidistant | integrated, about 1000 Hz | 4 |
| Snapdragon Flight | 2 global-shutter, 640×480, equidistant | about 500 Hz | 4 |

Board: AprilGrid 5×4, `tag_size` 0.075 m, `tag_spacing` 0.2, family `t36h11`.

Data and UZH's own Kalibr results: [UZH-FPV datasets page](https://fpv.ifi.uzh.ch/datasets/). Cite Delmerico et al.,
"Are We Ready for Autonomous Drone Racing? The UZH-FPV Drone Racing Dataset", ICRA 2019. License: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.

## Reading these results

- **One Basalt solve is left out.** On `davis_indoor_forward` with these weights, Basalt gives a 120 mm lever and an accel
  scale of 0.84, so its Davis column has 3 of 4 bags. At Basalt's default noise that bag is plausible, but
  `snapdragon_indoor_45` then fails with a broken spline.
- **Basalt's Davis distance stands out.** Its camera-to-IMU distance is 33.6 mm, against 23.2 mm from Kalibr and 22.8 mm
  from MVIS on the same 3 bags. We haven't established why. At default noise Basalt's lever z is 14–25 mm.
- **Each device's 4 bags form one family**, because the camera and IMU are on the same device. That assumes the
  camera-IMU geometry stayed the same across recordings, which we can't confirm.
- **Snapdragon's time offset really does differ between bags**, with an sd near 4.7 ms in every tool, so a small difference
  between tools on that row says little about each bag.
- **Rotations aren't in this table**, the same as on the leaderboard, because the IMU models define the IMU frame differently.
