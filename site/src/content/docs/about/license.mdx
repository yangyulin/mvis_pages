## Toolbox

[`LICENSE`](https://github.com/openmvis/openmvis/blob/main/LICENSE). MVIS is
Copyright (C) 2026 Yulin Yang.

Under the GPL you may use, study, modify and share MVIS, for any purpose,
including commercial ones. In particular:

- **Research, teaching and company-internal use** are free. Running MVIS to
  calibrate your own devices, in a lab or in a company, needs no agreement.
- **Products that include or derive from MVIS** must themselves be released
  under GPL-3.0, with their source.
- **Calibration results are yours.** The GPL covers the code, not what it
  computes: intrinsics, extrinsics, time offsets, residuals, plots and reports
  you produce by running MVIS on your own recordings may be published anywhere,
  including on commercial product pages. Results computed on a dataset follow
  that dataset's licence.

**Commercial licence.** If you want to ship MVIS inside a closed-source product,
or cannot meet the GPL terms for another reason, a paid commercial licence is
available. The terms are in
[`COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`](https://github.com/openmvis/openmvis/blob/main/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md);
write to licensing@openmvis.com.

**Names.** You may say that results were computed with MVIS ("Calibrated with
MVIS", linking https://openmvis.com). Do not use the MVIS or OpenMVIS names or
logos in a way that suggests the project endorses or certifies your product
without written permission.

## This site

The site repository is
[`openmvis/mvis_pages`](https://github.com/openmvis/mvis_pages).

- **Code** (the Astro site, its components, scripts and the embedded MVIS
  report views): **GPL-3.0-only**, the same licence as the toolbox.
- **Text and figures** written for this site: **CC BY 4.0**. Reuse them with
  attribution to OpenMVIS and a link to the page.

Exceptions, which keep their own terms:

- **Third-party dataset clips and figures.** Clips and figures on the homepage,
  the example pages and the device pages that are made from a third-party
  recording carry that recording's licence, stated on the page:
  [TUM-VI](/benchmark/examples/tum-vi/) CC BY 4.0,
  [Monado MSD](/benchmark/examples/monado/) CC BY 4.0,
  [UZH-FPV](/benchmark/examples/uzh-fpv/) CC BY-NC-SA 3.0,
  GiLabs, [Looper](/benchmark/examples/looper-insight9/) and
  [VI-Rig](/reference/datasets/) CC BY-NC 4.0, and the Dibuli maker example
  data under Dibuli's terms.
- **Product photos** on the device pages belong to their makers. Each is
  credited with a link to its source and is not covered by this licence.
- **The replay pack** on [Record the dataset](/guides/calibration-motion/#replay-it-yourself)
  states its own split: the guide motions, rigs and results CC BY 4.0, the two
  real-example trajectories CC BY-NC 4.0.

If you are the maker and want a photo changed or removed, write to
contact@openmvis.com.

## Datasets

Each dataset page states its licence and citation — see
[Datasets](/reference/datasets/).

- **Our own recordings** (VI-Rig and Looper Insight 9, hosted on Hugging Face):
  **CC BY-NC 4.0**. Access is gated with automatic approval, so you accept the
  terms once and download. Commercial use is possible by request: write to
  contact@openmvis.com.
- **Converted third-party sets** that we host (format-converted copies of
  CC BY sets): **CC BY 4.0**, with the original authors credited on the page.
- **TUM-VI** and other third-party datasets we only reference: their own
  licences (TUM-VI is CC BY 4.0). Download them from their owners.
- **Contributed recordings** in
  [`calibench-vi/community`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/community)
  are CC BY-NC 4.0. See [Submit a dataset](/contribute/submit-dataset/).

Results computed on a dataset follow that dataset's licence: results from the
CC BY-NC 4.0 data may not be used commercially without permission.

[Kalibr](https://github.com/ethz-asl/kalibr) and
[Basalt](https://gitlab.com/VladyslavUsenko/basalt) appear in the benchmark as
reference tools. We only run them; we do not distribute them.
