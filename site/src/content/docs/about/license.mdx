## MVIS toolbox

MVIS is dual-licensed. The copyright holder is named in the header of each
source file.

- **GPL-3.0-only** ([`LICENSE`](https://github.com/openmvis/openmvis/blob/main/LICENSE)):
  free for everyone, companies included, for research, teaching and internal
  use. If you distribute software that includes or is derived from MVIS, you
  must release it under GPL-3.0.
- **Commercial licence**
  ([`COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`](https://github.com/openmvis/openmvis/blob/main/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md)):
  for closed-source products, or whenever you cannot meet the GPL-3.0 terms.
  Write to licensing@openmvis.com.

### Calibration results

The GPL covers the MVIS code, not what it computes.

- **Your recordings:** results you compute on your own recordings
  (intrinsics, extrinsics, time offsets, residuals, plots, reports) are yours.
  You may publish them anywhere, including on commercial product pages, without
  a commercial licence.
- **Our datasets:** results computed on a dataset follow that dataset's licence.
  Results on the CC BY-NC 4.0 VI-Rig data may not be used commercially without a
  dataset licence.

### The MVIS name

You may say that results were computed with MVIS, for example "Calibrated with
MVIS" with a link to https://openmvis.com. Do not use the MVIS or OpenMVIS names
or logos in a way that suggests the project endorses or certifies your product
without written permission.

### Contributing code

Pull requests come with a contributor licence agreement, stated in
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](https://github.com/openmvis/openmvis/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
It lets the maintainer also offer your contribution under the commercial
licence.

## This site

The site repository is
[`openmvis/mvis_pages`](https://github.com/openmvis/mvis_pages).

- **Code** (the Astro site, its components, scripts and the embedded MVIS
  report views): **GPL-3.0-only**, the same licence as the toolbox.
- **Text and figures** written for this site: **CC BY 4.0**. Reuse them with
  attribution to OpenMVIS and a link to the page.

Exceptions, which keep their own terms:

- **Third-party dataset clips and figures.** Clips and figures on the homepage,
  the example pages and the device pages that are made from a third-party
  recording carry that recording's licence, stated on the page:
  [TUM-VI](/benchmark/examples/tum-vi/) CC BY 4.0,
  [Monado MSD](/benchmark/examples/monado/) CC BY 4.0,
  [UZH-FPV](/benchmark/examples/uzh-fpv/) CC BY-NC-SA 3.0,
  GiLabs, [Looper](/benchmark/examples/looper-insight9/) and
  [VI-Rig](/datasets/) CC BY-NC 4.0, and the Dibuli maker example
  data under Dibuli's terms.
- **Product photos** on the device pages belong to their makers. Each is
  credited with a link to its source and is not covered by this licence.
- **The replay pack** on [Record the dataset](/guides/calibration-motion/#replay-it-yourself)
  states its own split: the guide motions, rigs and results CC BY 4.0, the two
  real-example trajectories CC BY-NC 4.0.

If you are the maker and want a photo changed or removed, write to
contact@openmvis.com.

## Datasets

All datasets are in the [Calibench](https://huggingface.co/calibench-vi)
organisation on Hugging Face. The licence is set per dataset; see
[Datasets](/datasets/) and the
[dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets).

- **VI-Rig recordings (ours):** `virig-4imus-3cams`, `virig-4imus-3cams-aruco`,
  `virig-4imus-4cams` and `virig-5imus-4cams` are
  [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Access is
  gated: accept the terms on the Hub once, then download. Free for research,
  teaching and other non-commercial use with attribution. Commercial use needs a
  separate licence: write to licensing@openmvis.com.
- **Partner recordings:** `looper-insight9` and `gilabs-examples` have the same
  CC BY-NC 4.0 terms, with the partner's approval. Anyone, a company included,
  may run a calibration tool on the non-commercial datasets to produce results
  for the benchmark, and publish those results with attribution.
- **Converted third-party sets:** the calibration recordings under `converted/`
  (Monado SLAM datasets MIC, MGC and MOC, and the TUM-VIE frame cameras) stay
  under their original [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
  commercial use included. Each bag carries a `.provenance.json` with its source
  address. Cite the original dataset.
- **Other third-party datasets:** we do not re-host them. `mvis datasets` and the
  recipes on the dataset card download them from their owners, under the owners'
  terms. TUM-VI, for example, is CC BY 4.0.
- **Contributed recordings:** recordings in
  [`calibench-vi/community`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/community)
  are CC BY-NC 4.0. See [Submit a dataset](/contribute/submit-dataset/).
