## Toolbox

The Open MVIS toolbox ([rpng/mvis](https://github.com/rpng/mvis)) is released
under the **GNU General Public License v3.0**. The full text ships as `LICENSE`
in the repository.

GPL-3.0 is copyleft: distributing a modified version, or software that links
against it, means releasing that work under GPL-3.0 as well. If you are
embedding the toolbox in a product, check this before you build on it.

## This site

The site ([rpng/mvis_pages](https://github.com/rpng/mvis_pages)) — its source,
content, and generated pages — is released under the **MIT License**. Prose and
figures here can be reused with attribution.

## Datasets

Released datasets carry their own licences, stated on each dataset's page.
Contributed datasets need a licence permitting redistribution and derivative
results; CC BY 4.0 is the usual choice. See
[Submit a dataset](/contribute/submit-dataset/).

## Third-party components

Open MVIS builds on software under its own terms — GTSAM (BSD),
OpenCV (Apache 2.0), OpenVINS (GPL-3.0), apriltags-cpp, and ROS. Their licences
apply to their own code; consult each project.

## Citing

Citation details are on [Contributors & citation](/about/contributors/).
Datasets have their own DOIs and citations.
