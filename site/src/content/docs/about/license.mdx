- **Datasets:** their own licences; contributed recordings are CC BY-NC 4.0
- **Third-party components:** their own licences

## Toolbox

MVIS declares the **MIT License** in its `pyproject.toml`.

MIT is permissive. You may:

- use it
- modify it
- redistribute it, including in closed-source products

The condition: the copyright notice and licence text travel with it.

The declaration lives in package metadata. The repository does not yet carry a
`LICENSE` file with the full text. Until it does, treat this page as

:::

## This site

[`yangyulin/mvis_pages`](https://github.com/yangyulin/mvis_pages) — source,
content, and generated pages — is released under the **MIT License**. Prose and
figures here can be reused with attribution.

Two exceptions:

- **Third-party clips and figures:** clips and figures made from a third-party
  recording, such as those on the sensor pages, carry that recording's licence,
  stated on the page.
- **Product photos:** product photos on the sensor pages belong to their makers.
  Each is credited with a link to its source and isn't covered by this licence.

If you are the maker and want a photo changed or removed, write to
contact@openmvis.com.
