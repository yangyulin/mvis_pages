Where MVIS sits relative to the toolboxes most people already use. Kalibr is
mature and widely validated; MVIS is not trying to displace it on the
single-IMU case it does well.

## What is actually different

**No ROS.** Pure Python via `pip`, on Windows, macOS, and Linux. Bags are read
with `rosbags`. For anyone calibrating outside a ROS environment this is the
whole story.

**More than one IMU.** Kalibr and Basalt model a single IMU; a four-IMU rig has
to be calibrated pairwise and composed, which accumulates error and throws away
the correlations. MVIS puts every IMU in one graph.

**It measures driver artefacts instead of working around them.** `t_ga`
quantifies the gyro-vs-accel timing offset a driver's interpolation introduces —
the T265 reads −6.2 ms, stable to ~0.2 ms. Kalibr has no equivalent; the usual
alternative is to discard the sensor.

**Validation is a command, not a habit.** `mvis roundtrip` gives a PASS/FAIL
that requires IMU intrinsics to collapse *and* geometry to hold. Nothing
comparable ships with the others.

**Discrete-time.** Kalibr and Basalt fit a B-spline; MVIS places state nodes
at `state_hz`. The trade-off is in
[Continuous-time refinement](/concepts/continuous-time/).

## Where the others are still ahead

**Maturity.** Kalibr has a decade of use across the field. MVIS's 228-run
benchmark is strong evidence, not the same thing as that.

**Ecosystem.** Kalibr's target generators, tutorials, and community answers have
no MVIS equivalent yet.

**Continuous-time.** If your sensors are genuinely asynchronous at high rate, a
B-spline handles it natively; MVIS does not yet offer it.

## Agreement with Kalibr

Not a rival claim — a validation. On TUM-VI raw IMU bags, `omni_radtan` and
`equidistant` reproduce the Kalibr camera–IMU extrinsics to **≤2 mm lever,
≤0.15 mm stereo baseline, ≤1 ms time offset**. Camera parameters match the
published TUM-VI and Intel T265 calibrations to under 0.6% on focals.

Kalibr output also reads directly (`mvis.io.kalibr`), so it is a natural
source of initial values — refinement from a Kalibr camchain is a supported
workflow, not a migration.

## Which to use

- **One IMU, one or two cameras, ROS available** → Kalibr.
- **Multiple IMUs, or a gyro-only auxiliary** → MVIS.
- **No ROS on the machine** → MVIS.
- **Rolling shutter** → MVIS, or Kalibr's fork.
- **Mixed camera models on one rig** → MVIS.
- **You need to prove the calibration is right** → MVIS's round-trip.
