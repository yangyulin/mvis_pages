The benchmark, the [Datasets](/datasets/) section and the [Device wiki](/devices/)
rest on recordings that other people made and chose to share. Thank you.

Please cite each source you use. [Contributors & citation](/about/contributors/#citing-the-datasets)
explains how, and the
[dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets) lists the
paper for each set.

## Public datasets

Research groups that publish calibration recordings openly:

| Provider | Datasets we use | Used for |

| [Collabora](https://huggingface.co/datasets/collabora/monado-slam-datasets) | Monado SLAM datasets MIC, MGC and MOC | [results](/benchmark/results/monado-mgc/), [examples](/benchmark/examples/monado/), the [Valve Index](/devices/valve-index/), [HP Reverb G2](/devices/hp-reverb-g2/) and [Samsung Odyssey+](/devices/samsung-odyssey-plus/) pages |
| [Robotics and Perception Group, University of Zurich](https://fpv.ifi.uzh.ch/) | UZH-FPV | [results](/benchmark/results/uzh-fpv/), [examples](/benchmark/examples/uzh-fpv/) |
| Hilti, Trimble and the University of Oxford | [Hilti-Trimble SLAM Challenge 2026](https://github.com/Hilti-Research/hilti-trimble-slam-challenge-2026) | the [Insta360 ONE RS 1-Inch 360](/devices/insta360-one-rs-1inch-360/) page |
| [Hilti](https://hilti-challenge.com/) | Hilti 2022, Hilti 2023 handheld | download recipes |

The Monado and TUM-VIE frame-camera recordings are CC BY 4.0, which lets us host
converted copies under
[`converted/`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/calibench-vi/datasets/tree/main/converted).
We link to every other set at its original host.

## Device wiki

Makers and partners who shared recordings of their devices:

- **[LooperRobotics](https://looper-robotics.com/):** the hand-held and
  robot-arm recordings on the [Insight 9](/devices/looper-insight9/) page and in
  the Looper [example](/benchmark/examples/looper-insight9/). Thanks to Qinrui Yan
  and Junjie Tang, who collected them and supported sharing this work openly.
- **[General Intelligence Labs](https://www.gilabs.xyz/):** the
  [GI Ego1](/devices/gi-ego1/) and [GI EGO1GS](/devices/gi-ego1gs/) recordings,
  and `gilabs-examples` on the Hub.
- **[ALG Tech](https://www.aili-light.com/):** the
  [AVS111C-Y](/devices/alg-avs111c/) and [AVS115D-Y](/devices/alg-avs115d/)
  recordings.
- **[Tujian](https://tujian.tech):** the [CyperStereo](/devices/cyperstereo/)
  recording.
- **[DIBULI](https://dibuli.com):** the public example recordings behind the
  [Prism-A4L](/devices/dibuli-prism-a4l/) page.
- **The suppliers of the [Hampo module](/devices/hampo/) and the
  [XQ20 headset](/devices/xq20/):** the recordings on those pages.

Product photos on the device pages come from the makers' product pages and are
credited under each photo.
