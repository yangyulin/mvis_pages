Use this page to record a take the solver can use. You need the board from
[step 1](/guides/board-setup/). You get one bag per take.

- **Takes from step 1:** the board, set up and measured.
- **Hands on to step 3:** the bags, for [camera-only calibration](/guides/camera-calibration/).

A calibration can only find what the motion shows it. Our 60 s motion below excites every axis. It also
sweeps the board across the whole image.

We built it from a real hand-held recording with our Looper Insight 9. We checked it with a **noisy
digital twin**: a simulated copy of the rig that we record, solve and compare with the truth. A motion is
good if the twin still converges on every calibration parameter. Converges means the solve settles on a
value.

## 1. What a good take has

- **About 60 s long.** 50 s also works.
- **Fill the frame.** Cover the image out to its corners. Distortion is only pinned down where the board
  was seen.
- **Vary distance and angle.** Move in and out, and turn the rig about every axis.
- **Avoid motion blur.** Use enough light for a short exposure (about 2 ms).
- **Rest at both ends.** Hold still for about 3 s at the start and the end.

## <span id="step-by-step" class="cm-anchor"></span>3. Step by step

Do four moves in order, with a short rest at each end. Blend each move into the next. Keep a comfortable
hand pace: about 0.4 m/s and 1.9 rad/s at most.

| # | Move | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Rest | 0–3 s | Hold still in front of the board, count 3. |
| 1 | Left–right + turn left-right | 3–20 s | Swing left and right around the board, 2 times. At each end, turn the camera a little past facing the board, so the board slides to the left or right edge of the picture. Turn round sharply. |
| 2 | Up–down + tilt up-down | 20–32 s | Move up and down, 2 times. Tilt the camera a little past the board, so the board reaches the top or bottom edge of the picture. |
| 3 | Back–forth + twist | 32–47 s | Move in to 0.22 m and out to 0.52 m, 3 times. Twist the camera about its view axis like a steering wheel, one way coming in and the other going out. Hold the board off-centre so the twist carries it into the corners. |
| 4 | Loop + twist | 47–57 s | Move round a loop parallel to the board, about 43 cm across, 2 times. Turn the camera a little against the loop and twist it a little, once per loop, so the board runs into every corner. |
| 5 | Rest | 57–60 s | Hold still, count 3. |

- **Vary the speed** on every sweep: push through the middle, brake into the ends.
- **Keep most of the board in view.** An AprilGrid may run partly off the edge; a chessboard may not.
- **Don't add shake.** The natural wobble of a hand is all the motion needs.

## Motions that fail

Avoid these motions. Each leaves some parameters loose: the solve cannot pin them down.

- **Fast shaking from one spot, face-on:** the lens distortion stays loose.
- **One slow axis at a time, never tilted:** lever arm (the camera–IMU position offset), time offsets and IMU scales
  stay loose.
- **A missing rotation** (no tilt, no twist, or only turning left-right): the matching gyro and accelerometer scales,
  or the whole camera–IMU rotation, cannot be found.
- **The board always centred:** the image edges are never seen, so the distortion stays loose.

## How we judged it

The twin is a simulated Looper Insight 9 in front of the board. It has realistic corner and IMU noise,
motion blur and dropped detections.

Each motion is solved on 3 noise seeds plus a noise-free run. In each solve t_ga (the time offset between the
gyroscope and the accelerometer) is estimated. The camera intrinsics (focal length, image centre and lens distortion) are free.

A motion is **good** if, on all 22 parameters:

- every solve converges;
- the error stays within its uncertainty;
- the uncertainty is at most 3× that of a well-excited 80 s reference motion.

The 22 parameters are the camera–IMU rotation, lever arm, td (the camera–IMU time offset), t_ga, gyro and
accelerometer scales, and the camera intrinsics.

To run the same check on your own calibration, see [step 7: digital-twin check](/guides/simulation/).

## Replay it yourself

Run our motions through your own simulator and calibration tool with the
[replay pack](/downloads/calib-motion-replay.zip) (zip, 3.2 MB). It holds:

- **the four motions** at 200 Hz (IMU pose, ideal gyro and accelerometer);
- **the two twin rigs** (camera, IMU, truth, noise and detection rules);
- **our twin results** with the pass rule.

Its README gives the frames, units and replay steps. The twin itself ran on MVIS, which is not public yet.
